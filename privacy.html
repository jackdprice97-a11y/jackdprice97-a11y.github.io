Privacy Policy

This policy covers all games by Jack Price:

  • The Arrr-gon Trail (iPhone, iPad and Android)
  • Rimstone (iPhone and iPad)

Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version

The games themselves collect nothing about you: there are no accounts, and your progress stays on your device. The apps show ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

What stays on your device

Each game stores your progress and settings only on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. In The Arrr-gon Trail that means your saved voyage, settings, Legends and Graves; in Rimstone, your settings, the creatures you have beaten and the balls you have unlocked. This is never sent anywhere. Deleting a game deletes it.

Ads

The apps use Google AdMob to show ads:

  • The Arrr-gon Trail: ads between voyages, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to save a crew member.
  • Rimstone: a full-screen ad between some games.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking when iOS asks; on Android you can reset or delete it in Settings › Privacy › Ads), your approximate location from your IP address, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. This is not linked to your name or any account in the games.

In the UK, EU and some other regions you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in the game:

  • The Arrr-gon Trail: title screen › Settings › Ad privacy choices
  • Rimstone: title screen › Extras › Privacy

You can also turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Internet

The games play fully offline. When you are online, the apps contact Google only to load ads.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and the games themselves collect no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the apps are. New games are added to the list at the top when they are released.
