The Arrr-gon Trail

A pixel-art pirate voyage roguelike for iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store

The voyage

Pick a captain, sign on a crew and stock the hold, then sail 2,500 leagues toward the legendary Arrr-gon. Ration food and limes, fight or flee pirates, steer through storms, dig for treasure, and face the Kraken at the end of the trail. Death is permanent; every run takes about two hours and saves as you go.
